[*]October 27th, 2026
Nephtan
Announcements:

The latest fixes are now live, with improvements to shopping, rewards, player-city services, and bandaging.

Shopping and rewards:

- NPC vendor purchases have extra safeguards against duplicate charges and incorrect totals on large orders. Gold, bank-check, and resource deductions also reject invalid amounts.
- Buying a bulk order deed from a vendor's book now confirms the deed reaches your backpack before taking payment. If delivery fails, your gold and the book's entry are preserved.
- Quest and PvP reward shops now recheck eligibility and the displayed offer before spending credits. Outdated or repeated claims are rejected, and failed deliveries trigger a credit refund.

Player-city services:

- Paid corpse retrieval now checks your corpse, the quoted fee, your eligibility, distance, and remaining uses before taking gold or using one of your allowed retrievals.
- City tax changes and treasury withdrawals recheck who currently has permission, so an old menu cannot be used after a mayor change.

Bandaging:

- Cancelled or replaced bandages can no longer finish late, disrupt a newer bandage, or apply the same healing twice.
- Where turn-based combat is enabled, bandages now finish correctly as turns advance, including healing another character. Switching between turn-based and regular combat preserves the remaining bandage time.

Normal shop prices, reward costs, healing amounts, and bandage times are unchanged.

Please report missing purchases or rewards, incorrect charges, or bandages that fail to complete. Include what you were doing and any message shown.

Good hunting!

Server changes since the August 9th announcement:

Character progress and skills:

- Region-entry checks flag inconsistent data without resetting your skills, stats, or profession.

- Origin, boost, and Ether caps are consistent; supported saved boosts survive reloads.
- Staff-returned characters can reselect an origin under its normal reset rules; stale menu responses are rejected.
- Titan of Ether completion checks the quest item and trials, and grants its cap bonus and rewards only once.

Champions, creatures, and encounters:

- Champion Power Scrolls and Skulls can be awarded on every playable facet under the same eligibility rules.
- Champion-wave Scrolls of Transcendence now use the same chance and value range across playable facets.
- Town guards can die instead of instantly healing; normal kill awards remain disabled.
- Draguuls now deal 75% physical and 25% fire damage. The old incomplete profile is repaired on load; customized profiles are preserved.
- Dungeon, cave, and bard-dungeon encounter checks now run every five minutes instead of every two and a half minutes.

Magic:

- Sanctify's buff countdown now matches the spell's actual duration.
- Repeated Research Enchant and Sneak casts no longer add extra logout work. Old Enchant timers cannot end newer enchantments.
- Create Food removes conjured food, drink, blood, or brains that cannot fit in your backpack, with clearer delivery messages.

Housing, cities, and games:

- Townhouse and rental regions cover the correct floors, restoring house commands and tools. Tenant commands take priority over the parent house.
- City voting rechecks eligibility, distance, and the current election before accepting a vote.
- New or loaded Liar's Dice items preserve the shared game. Stale turn timers cannot warn or remove players.

Stability and player references:

- Paced item and property updates reduce crowded-area disconnect risk during travel and reconnect. Large scenes may fill in gradually.
- Wanted reports batch disk writes while preserving reports and Town Crier notices.
- New references cover stats and caps, Searching/Hiding/Stealth, Begging, traps, game systems, and a FAQ drawn from Discord questions.

Please report unexpected skill changes, missing rewards, broken house commands, or crowded-area disconnects.

[*]October 10th, 2026

Announcements:

ClassicUO and ClassicAssist updates are now available through the Confictura launcher. Please run the launcher and let it finish updating before playing.

These updates bring the following changes since August 8th, 2026.

ClassicUO:

- Open bags should no longer close just because you change facets.
- Added support for creature and mount animations supplied through legacy animation patches, including animations missing from the base animation files.
- Corrected handling of terrain and scenery patches sent by the server.
- Added support for shard-supplied item and ground artwork, interface artwork, sounds, and text. When the shard provides these custom assets, they can be reloaded from Options > Experimental without restarting the client.

ClassicAssist:

- Object Inspector windows no longer block the rest of the assistant, so you can leave an inspection window open while continuing to work.
- Container-viewer filters now have an improved tree layout for nested groups, making it easier to arrange more detailed AND/OR/NOT filtering rules.
- Added WaitForRemoveObject for macro authors. It waits for the server to remove a specified item or creature from view, with an optional timeout.
- Includes an upstream fix for crashes when closing the ClassicAssist launcher.
- Added optional MCP server (AI agents) support for compatible AI tools. It can create, edit, run and stop macros, call macro commands, and inspect information such as your character, inventory, journal, open menus, and agent settings. The local server is off by default and must be enabled in ClassicAssist's Options each session.

Please report bags that still close during travel, misplaced riders, missing animations, or problems with ClassicAssist macros and container filters. Include what you were doing and the relevant location, mount, filter, or macro.

Good hunting!
