# Paladin reference toolkit review

Status: source review and policy reassessment in progress. No encounter change is authorized by this review. Reference-v1/v2 are preliminary policy experiments, not adequate evidence for tuning the encounter. A legal character manifest alone does not establish a competent combat policy.

The review covers the actual trained skills, equipped items, full Chivalry book, carried consumables, ordinary preparation choices and relevant player actions. It does not assume vanilla UO behavior or that OmniAI is a complete player model. The current reference has Knightship 100, effective Swords/Parry/Healing 125, Tactics/Anatomy/MagicResist 120, Focus/Alchemy 100, 50 maximum mana and a source-capped 40% Lower Mana Cost. New abilities must be exercised through ordinary player/client paths before their effectiveness is claimed.

## Healing, consumables, preparation and passives

- **Bandages are also a poison cure.** `Data/Scripts/Items/Trades/Misc/Bandage.cs` uses `(Healing-30)/50 -Poison.Level*0.1 -Slips*0.02`, with Healing/Anatomy at least60. At125 Healing this starts at1.6 for deadly and1.5 for lethal before slips. A completion on a poisoned patient takes the cure branch instead of ordinary wound healing. Self delay at Dexterity100 is6seconds under AOS; debuffs can lengthen it. v1/v2 start bandages for injury but do not deliberately use this cure route or coordinate a near-complete bandage with potion/spell use. Reassess both.
- **Greater Cure is unusually reliable for this build.** `BasePotion.EnhancePotions` returns the gear contribution capped50 plus its skill bonus; at this Alchemy it contributes30. `BaseCurePotion.DoCure` scales the probability itself. With AOS, no potion-enhancement gear and this skill, the95%/75% base chances become123.5%/97.5% for deadly/lethal. Thus a deadly cure is certain by the random comparison, while lethal retains a2.5% failure chance. Cleanse By Fire is another useful route, not automatically a safer cure. The source's skill thresholds use `Alchemy.Fixed`; do not restate them as unverified whole-skill thresholds.
- **Greater Heal** cannot substitute for curing poison. `GreaterHealPotion` has20–25 base healing and a10-second reuse delay; `BaseHealPotion` applies `PlayerLevelMod` and potion scaling. At this setup's multiplier2 and enhancement30, that requests52–65 healing before clipping. Potions in this pack do not require freeing a hand; the inherited default is false and these heal/cure classes do not override it. Item/cast legality still applies during control effects.
- **Orange Petals** provide the ordinary five-minute protection for poison levels below3; they do not protect against deadly/lethal. **Brood Antivenom** is separate prevention with three eligible charges, five-minute expiry and two-minute recipient reuse; it does not cure an existing poison. The no-antivenom reference must remain genuinely without it.
- **Strength, agility and stamina potions** were not stocked in v1/v2. This is a significant preparation omission. Greater Strength/Agility each request an offset of `Scale(PlayerLevelMod(20))`, which is52 in this setup, for the unscaled two-minute duration. `PlayerMobile` caps each effective stat at150 under ML/Player access: this reference can therefore reach Strength150 and Dexterity150, not182/152. With unchanged gear that implies health325, stamina300 and a3.5-second self bandage delay rather than6seconds; these derived expectations still need native confirmation. Total Refresh requests a full stamina proportion before enhancement/player scaling and is clipped by the ordinary stamina cap. Evaluate carry weight, refill need and actual acquisition before freezing the revised loadout. Do not silently introduce them mid-run. Traces: `Data/Scripts/Items/Potions/Standard/{Strength Potions,Agility Potions,Refresh Potions}/` and `Data/Scripts/Mobiles/Base/PlayerMobile.cs:1665`.
- **Parry, Anatomy, Tactics, MagicResist, Focus, Alchemy and equipment procs/regen** are used passively through normal source combat. Record effective caps, mana regeneration, leech and stamina behavior; do not assume raw DCI/SSI sums equal benefits. Divine Fury modifies DCI before its45 cap, so this heavily overcapped suit may absorb that penalty.
- **Food/thirst, positioning, targeting, retreat, range/LOS and supply limits** belong in preparation/policy. v1/v2 begin fed at20 (the food source cap) and below carrying capacity. They have no active-meditation preparation, escape rune, alternate weapon or pet. Other untrained schools/skills must not be granted to make the test pass. An audit should explain unavailable options rather than pretend they were exercised.

Additional traces: `Data/Scripts/Items/Potions/BasePotion.cs`; `Data/Scripts/Items/Potions/Standard/Cure Potions/{BaseCurePotion,GreaterCurePotion}.cs`; `Data/Scripts/Items/Potions/Standard/Heal Potions/{BaseHealPotion,GreaterHealPotion}.cs`; `Data/Scripts/Trades/Gardening/MiscItems/OrangePetals.cs`; `Data/Scripts/System/Misc/{AOS,RegenRates,Settings}.cs`; `Data/Scripts/Custom/Champions/Outbreak/BroodAntivenom.cs`.

## OmniAI comparison and limits

`Data/Scripts/Custom/ThirdParty/OmniAI/OmniAI Shared.cs` sorts Magery/Necromancy/Knightship/Healing by effective skill for its healing choice. Its Knightship branch chooses Cleanse By Fire when poisoned and Close Wounds otherwise. Its Healing branch constructs `BandageContext` directly; this NPC path is not the player's stock-consuming item/target/BeginHeal path. With this reference's125 Healing versus100 Knightship, the sorted choice may prefer bandaging first.

`OmniAI Knightship.cs` considers Remove Curse, Holy Light, Dispel Evil, Divine Fury and Consecrate Weapon, with random selection. Its Remove Curse check recognizes negative magic stat modifiers and also guesses from an opponent's Necromancy skill. Its generic `UseWeaponStrike` in `OmniAI Shared.cs` selects only primary or secondary moves, using hardcoded skill thresholds rather than reviewing all five configured slot requirements. That is broader than the v1/v2 player policy but incomplete for this sword. No Enemy of One selection was found in this package's Knightship decision path. Do not copy NPC resource behavior or probabilistic guesses as verified player decisions.

## Reassessed policy direction before the next freeze

The next reference should stock and validate finite Greater Strength/Agility and Total Refresh, prepare a legitimate retreat destination, and confirm carrying capacity. Preserve raw stats, trained skills, named gear and source costs; do not refill resources or grant powers mid-fight. Any revised loadout gets its own explicit pre-combat manifest.

Use defender-first targeting and the now-tested connected short navigation legs. Maintain actual Consecrate/Enemy of One/Divine Fury effect state instead of blind recasting, account for Enemy of One's type change/vulnerability at the boss, and reserve mana for survival. Assess Dispel Evil for emergency add pressure, not Mephitis fear. Assess Holy Light for multiple nearby legal targets and Double Whirlwind for adjacent groups with sufficient stamina, including its real stamina assignment. Compare Fire Strike/Armor Ignore through native hits before selecting a single-target priority. Double Strike's higher mana and Death Blow's costly flat1.5 scalar are reasons to evaluate or omit them deliberately, rather than pretend they do not exist.

Curing must include all three available routes: a near-complete bandage when safely affordable, Cleanse By Fire when health/mana/casting conditions permit, and the highly reliable Greater Cure fallback for urgency, control, low mana or spell failure. Keep healing/cure restrictions and actual potion cooldowns. Remove significant curses/mortal wounds when actionable; self Remove Curse cannot start while ordinarily paralyzed. Noble Sacrifice remains excluded from a strictly solo run with no eligible ally, but belongs in cooperative testing. Sacred Journey is not executable without a real validated destination. These are requirements for a policy to validate and freeze, not an already accepted replacement macro.

The user subsequently approved removing only the inherited outbreak breath and adapting crowd qualification. Those are separately verified scoped design changes; they do not replace this full-toolkit testing requirement or authorize general player/NPC ability repairs.
