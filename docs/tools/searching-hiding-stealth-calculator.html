Hidden traps, doors, chests, and passive discovery

An eligible item inside the active Search's actual full or half square has no second PvP-style contest. Passive hidden-chest discovery is a different roll made when walking over the marker.

Active item inclusion—Same radius probability as the selected Search distance.
Passive Search roll—Exact Searching / 126.
Night Sight fallback—Runs only when passive Searching fails.
Combined passive discovery—Exact Search, then Night Sight fallback.
Hidden-chest search level—

Active eligible types are BaseTrap, configured hidden-door art, HiddenTrap, and HiddenChest. Active Search deletes a found HiddenChest after creating its reward; passively crossing one deletes the marker even on a failed discovery roll. For active Search, the broad reward split is 2/3 currency/resources and 1/3 hidden box; region and further random branches determine the final item.
