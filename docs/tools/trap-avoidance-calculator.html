What detection does not do

  • Particles and messages reveal the location; the trap item is not deleted or disabled.
  • Night Sight is not a passive trap-resistance roll in active Searching.
  • The Remove Trap handler contains a HiddenTrap target path, but detection does not make the invisible item a normal client target.
  • Spell-casting chances and resource costs are outside this avoidance calculator.
How the server calculates this
  • Data/Scripts/Items/Traps/HiddenTrap.cs — CheckTrapAvoidance: common wall/floor sequence, the literal HiddenTrap setting gate, orb ownership, Luck, pole charges, and the post-trigger saving throws.
  • Data/Scripts/System/Misc/Settings.cs — FloorTrapTrigger and Data/Scripts/System/Misc/Players.cs — LuckyPlayer: the checked-in 20 setting and the capped, integer-truncated Luck roll.
  • Data/Scripts/Items/Magical/Artifacts/Artifact_DetectiveBoots.cs, Artifact_GrayMouserCloak.cs, Artifact_TorchOfTrapFinding.cs, Data/Scripts/Items/Potions/Elixirs/BaseElixir.cs — Buff, Data/Scripts/Items/Potions/BasePotion.cs — EnhancePotions, and Data/Scripts/Items/Wands/2nd Circle/MagicUntrapStaff.cs: optional Remove Trap and Magery scenario modifiers.
  • Data/System/Source/Mobile.cs — CheckSkill / CheckTargetSkill and Data/Scripts/System/Skills/SkillCheck.cs: any requested maximum of at least 100 is replaced by 126; chance is (value − min) / (max − min).
  • Data/System/Source/Skills.cs — Skill.Value: formulas read total skill, including applicable skill modifiers. Power scrolls set only Skill.Cap.
  • Data/Scripts/Items/Traps/TenFootPole.cs — TapChance: material weight maps to 50–92%. A charge is removed only on a successful pole save.
  • Data/Scripts/Magic/Magery/Magery 2nd/RemoveTrap.cs and Data/Scripts/Items/Traps/TrapWand.cs: the self-targeted spell creates the owned, 30-minute orb and sets its power from Magery.
  • Data/Scripts/Items/Containers/TrapableContainer.cs — ExecuteTrap: level threshold, Remove Trap, Luck, discrete orb/pole rolls, and post-trigger mitigation.
  • Data/Scripts/System/Skills/RemoveTrap.cs: active container disarming uses the same level-based check and fails without setting off the trap.
  • Data/Scripts/System/Skills/Searching.cs, Magery Reveal, Shinobi EagleEye, Jedi MindsEye, and GemOfSeeing: detection radii and reveal-only behavior.
  • Data/Scripts/Magic/Research/Spells/Theurgy/ResearchAirWalk.cs and ResearchSpell.DamagingSkill: supported trap bypasses and the duration calculation.
  • Data/Scripts/Quests/Shadowlords/GemImmortality.cs and Data/Scripts/Magic/Research/Spells/Death/ResearchWithstandDeath.cs — JewelInPocket: the trap classes that short-circuit when either item is present.
  • Data/Scripts/Magic/Witch/Effects/Phantasm.cs, BottleOfAcid.cs, and PlasmaTorch.cs: active container alternatives.

Important implementation quirks

125 means 126

Checks written as 0–125 are changed to 0–126. Total skill 125 therefore leaves a 0.7937% failure chance.

Floor setting is reversed in use

The checked-in setting says “20% trigger,” but CheckTrapAvoidance treats its successful 1–100 roll as avoidance.

Air Walk seconds

The displayed lore says minutes; the timer is constructed with seconds.

Flame-spurt proximity hit

Its separate OnMovement 1–10 damage path has no Remove Trap, orb, Luck, pole, Air Walk, or immortality check.

Scope

Results assume a living, non-blessed player character. They calculate the selected player’s own trigger check. Some traps can damage nearby characters after someone else triggers them; no probability is invented for that collateral event.

All magical protection percentages assume the effect or item already exists and is valid. Normal spell-casting success, mana, reagents, research materials, and line-of-sight checks are separate.
