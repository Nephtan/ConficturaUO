# Confictura player FAQ

**Discord knowledge review · October 10, 2026**

This guide consolidates the supplied getting-started, game-help, and general histories. Answers describe what those conversations support. **Community tip** means practical player advice, not a guaranteed rule. **Open** means the histories do not establish an answer. **Source check** identifies a limited check of this repository; it does not prove the live server has identical code or settings. Dated fixes and disputed claims are tracked in [Corrections and open questions](CORRECTIONS_AND_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).

Source labels link to the original Discord messages. They also appear in the [evidence register](evidence-register.json). The [starter guide](START_HERE.md) is the short route through this material. Specific destination and quest clues are collected in the optional [spoiler guide](SPOILERS.md).

## Client and basic controls

### Q001. Which program should I launch?

Use the launcher supplied with the current Confictura package. The April 2026 clarification names **Confictura.exe**; older posts tell players to launch ClassicUO.exe directly. Those instructions belong to different packages. Start from the current getting-started distribution, not a years-old download or hostname. [G44104](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1492930432323420220) [G44106](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1493398204425834597) [GS15](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1063677605192138842/1382510418018435242)

### Q002. The download looks old or broken. What should I try first?

Check the current getting-started download post and refresh the download page. A hard refresh resolved stale download information in one exchange. Back up profiles and macros before replacing a client installation. An old deleted Dropbox link is not proof that the shard is unavailable. ### Q003. Can I use Razor and ClassicAssist?

Staff described using both: Razor for simple recorded actions and ClassicAssist for more involved scripts. The exact configuration depends on the client package. If startup fails, identify the assistant and version involved before changing settings; disabling an assistant was a troubleshooting step in particular cases, not a requirement for everyone. ### Q004. Can I play on a phone, Linux, or Mac?

MobileUO users are present throughout the histories, although setup, small interfaces, and bank targeting caused difficulties. Linux users reported native ClassicUO with appropriate dependencies; those are historical configurations. A 2024 statement that Mac files were packaged is not a current installation guarantee: in September 2026, staff were unsure and did not confirm a packaged Mac version. Ask for platform-specific current instructions rather than assuming a desktop package works everywhere. ### Q005. How do I make the interface easier to read?

Look in **Options → Containers** for container scale, **Video** for game zoom, and **Fonts** for text choices. Use the resizable **World Map** from the top ribbon instead of relying on the small circular map. These settings do not necessarily scale every paperdoll or custom window. Windows display scaling was also discussed as a possible cause of display problems. [GH3087](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1387204540624932945) [GH3095](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1387291202390593599) [GH4937](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1481469310940483584)

### Q006. How do I talk to everyone in game?

Type `[c your message`. Entering `[c` alone opens the chat interface. Local speech and server chat are different. ### Q007. Where is the main help system?

Open **HELP on the paperdoll**. Read the skill information and the **Library** inside it. Single-click NPCs and choose **Talk**. Those conversations and opened lore books can be saved in the Library, so you can revisit information after leaving the NPC or dungeon. [GH2476](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1353821608636190851) [GH2478](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1353823547461603358) [G38879](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1273413962641440910)

### Q008. Is there a conventional quest log?

`[quests` covers only part of the content. Many objectives come from lore books, parchment clues, NPC dialogue, and items. Keep notes about the clue, dungeon, giver, and return location; an empty quest log does not mean you have no quests. [G14807](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1142082262507200583) [G7503](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1090620551917228104) [GH4410](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1436134137902661735)

### Q009. How do I inspect my character's situation?

Use **INFO** on the paperdoll for information such as fame, karma, hunger, and character status. Players also described clicking the guilty/status area to see relevant town restrictions. Check this before treating guards' reactions as random. [G1327](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1073676126821556233) [GH2998](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1386350425208524870) [GH4084](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1422724617545711656)

### Q010. Can I stop the Town Crier announcing my activities?

Staff directed players to the private-play option in **HELP → Settings**. This is useful for players who prefer quieter adventures. [G45403](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1543289791989415968)

### Q011. Why do characters' names appear twice?

Check whether both the client and Razor are displaying incoming names. A reported case was caused by both features being enabled. ## Character choices

### Q013. What is a sensible first character?

A standard human start is the least complicated learning path recommended by staff. Read every tarot description before choosing. Challenge starts can alter skills, luck, starting supplies, town access, and death penalties; a larger cap is not a free upgrade. [G28181](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1195117369396305972) [G28184](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1195118373516869723) [GH3807](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1405374053254172672)

### Q014. What do the standard, fugitive, and Alien starts change?

The histories describe a standard total skill cap of **1,000**, a Hanged Man/fugitive start of **1,300**, and the Star/Alien start of **4,000**. Alien starts sacrifice normal starting skills/resources and Luck benefits and have harsher resurrection consequences. Fugitive status changes town access. Some posts express skill points in tenths and say 10,000/40,000; do not mistake that notation for ten times the playable cap. Read the actual starting choices and current INFO display. [GH1028](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1163185583720505414) [GH1032](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1163187099575517196) [GH1033](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1163187320074276874) [GH2510](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1354548978317918329) [G28181](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1195117369396305972)

### Q015. Is Alien unable to learn magic?

No. Staff explicitly corrected that impression. However, a large total skill cap does not remove incompatibilities between particular magic systems. [GH1040](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1163199542498443305) [GH1041](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1163214010062213141) [GH2510](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1354548978317918329)

### Q016. What do nonhuman races trade away?

They can receive racial attributes and skill bonuses that change with character level, but have restrictions on appearance, equipment presentation, and ordinary mounts. Human characters offer familiar appearance and mount options. Read the race shelf in the starting tent, not just the tarot cards. ### Q017. How does a nonhuman character move at mounted speed?

Players were directed to **hiking boots** or appropriate artifacts such as Boots of Hermes. These are alternatives to ordinary mounts; they do not establish that every speed effect works in every dungeon. ### Q018. Is Neutral the same as PvE protection?

No. Staff explain that Neutral has no PvP protection and can be harmed by PvP characters; beneficial actions also differ between statuses. Read the actual consent choice carefully. Later discussion describes status choices as having one-way restrictions, so do not pick a tag assuming it can always be reset. [GH894](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1155146005780627567) [G44826](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1527753903809102027) [G44827](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1527753984969019575)

### Q019. Is roleplay mandatory?

The getting-started response says roleplay is optional. The community strongly favors exploration, roleplay, and respecting other players' enjoyment. A PvP tag is not permission to disregard that social expectation. [GS49](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1063677605192138842/1536375220418642021) [GH894](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1155146005780627567) [GH1420](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1217131708285980812)

### Q020. Can I change my build later?

Skills can be lowered through their locks while others rise, and soulstones provide another route to storing/transferring skill points. That flexibility does not imply every starting choice, quest choice, or artifact point purchase is reversible. [GH1220](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1215104776031961138) [GH4437](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1436921122724515871) [GS213](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1063677605192138842/1540680650443587686) [G45731](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1551703236677730305)

### Q021. Why can't I delete a new character immediately?

The getting-started discussion describes a seven-day deletion wait and alternatives such as using another available character slot. For an account or naming problem, ask staff; do not assume deleting the whole installation or starting over repeatedly will solve it. [GS148](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1063677605192138842/1536554098449453107) [GS150](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1063677605192138842/1536555076091515010) [GS152](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1063677605192138842/1536555913077329950)

### Q022. Why do guards hate my character?

Possible causes include criminal actions, murder counts, a fugitive background, race/alignment, and karma requirements. These are not interchangeable timers. Accidental area damage or autolooting a corpse without rights can also flag you. Inspect INFO and describe the action immediately before the change. ### Q023. Can a good title protect me after a karma-reversal trap?

Do not rely on the title. Players report that the changed karma can alter town access even while a familiar paladin-style title remains visible. Check actual fame/karma/status before entering guarded areas. [G46038](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1555093335096565891) [G39283](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1307801691713962055)

### Q024. Does my chosen appearance or play style change my build?

Staff describe the style choice as influencing the appearance and theme of loot, with barbarian and evil styles as examples. Treat that answer separately from race and tarot/background choices, which have mechanical tradeoffs. It is a way to influence the kinds of things your character finds, not evidence that selecting an appearance grants a combat bonus. ## Your first money and equipment

### Q025. What should I do before my first dungeon?

Get a usable weapon or working spell setup, healing supplies, food and water, and a ten-foot pole. Train some basic skills and Camping, bank resurrection money, and identify a return route. Pick a beginner area close enough to a town to make recovery practical. This is a synthesis of repeated starting advice, not a required build. [GS27](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1063677605192138842/1388583827437518930) [GS29](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1063677605192138842/1388584320528023552) [G9022](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1098286356297429033) [GH4713](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1454307699868176534)

### Q026. How can I make money when I cannot fight effectively?

Community options include harvesting cotton/flax for tailors, gathering and selling suitable resources, and beginning with simple crafted goods. Begging was a successful recovery route for one zero-skill Alien character. Check a buyer before committing to a large stockpile. Prices and profitability in old posts are examples, not guaranteed current rates. ### Q027. Why won't a vendor buy my items?

Each NPC has its own changing buy list and stock. Staff repeatedly describe rotation about once per in-game day, roughly **two real hours**. Try another appropriate vendor or town. A tailor refusing cloth today does not mean cloth is unsellable. [GH1235](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1215290842877788220) [GH1238](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1215498204037128202) [GH4392](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1435708524285001811)

### Q028. Which vendors should I try for ordinary resources?

Community guidance pairs meat with butchers, hides/leather with leatherworkers or suitable fur traders, arrows/feathers with bowyers, and logs with carpenters. Tailors are a starting point for cloth. The rotating list still applies. ### Q029. Where are tools, runes, or an empty spellbook?

Visit the relevant trade shops in more than one town. Mages can stock spellbooks and runes; absence at one mage is normal. Players looking for mining supplies had the same stock problem. Ask for the exact tool type, because an ore spade is not interchangeable with every mining tool. ### Q031. Can fugitives and unusual races use the public market?

A June 2026 explanation describes it as a shared vendor, meeting, crafting, and banking place regardless of race or fugitive status. Put them in your bank and double-click them. The player explanation says the converted gold appears in your backpack, so bank the gold again if you want it protected there. ### Q033. Why did my coins turn to lead?

The histories identify traps and magical pools as possible causes. Lead is not the same as copper/silver conversion currency. Staff describe ways to turn at least some lead back into gold, including Research magic; save it if you want to pursue that route. ### Q034. How do I make a bank check?

At a bank, say `check 100000` to make a 100,000-gold check if your bank balance covers it. Replace the number with the amount you need. [GH3348](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1392164823835480186) [GH3349](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1392164842353332306)

### Q035. Why do players from different towns appear in the same bank room?

A getting-started reply explains that entering banks from different places can lead into the same shared bank space. That explains meeting someone who entered elsewhere; it does not mean everyone's bank balance or stored belongings are shared. No. Some are decorative valuables or curiosities. Appraise them and use the appropriate buyer; a named scroll can be a sale item rather than a spell to cast. One player solved a scroll sale by taking it to an ordinary trading mage instead of the wrong mage NPC. ### Q037. How do I get something identified?

The histories distinguish usable unidentified gear, decorative valuables, unknown consumables, and unidentified artifacts. ## Traps and lost belongings

### Q073. What is the basic trap survival kit?

Build Remove Trap, carry a ten-foot pole, and use the self-targeted Magery trap-removal spell to make an orb when available. Luck can help on supported paths. Bank valuables you do not need. These are separate protections; armor alone cannot prevent inventory loss, fame/karma changes, or all other effects. ### Q074. Is 100 or 125 Remove Trap immunity?

No. **Source check:** the common skill roll uses effective skill divided by 126. Thus 100 is about **79.37%**, and 125 about **99.21%**, for that roll. Effective 126 guarantees that particular check, not protection from every trap family or another player's triggered damage. [GH5111](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1531328632331698187) [GH5129](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1531434988929683647) [C01](SOURCE_REVIEW.md#c01-trap-protection).

### Q075. Do pole, orb, Luck, and skill percentages add together?

No. They are successive chances to stop a qualifying trigger. If chances are `R`, `O`, `L`, and `P`, the chance that all four fail is `(1-R) × (1-O) × (1-L) × (1-P)`. Random hidden floors also have a separate setting check before that sequence; later effects have their own conditions. See the [full trap guide](../wiki/Traps_And_Avoidance.md). [C01](SOURCE_REVIEW.md#c01-trap-protection).

### Q076. Do I need to click the ten-foot pole constantly?

The maintained trap guide describes a carried backpack item used by the avoidance code. It is not a manual “tap every tile” action. Its material affects protection, and charges matter. Carrying several does not add several pole rolls. ### Q077. Is the self-protection spell called Magic Trap or Remove Trap?

### Q117. What does a throwing net do?

It captures an eligible uncontrolled tamable creature, adds **two slots** to that creature's requirement, and bonds it immediately. On success the net is consumed. Paragons/cursed creatures, untamables, already-controlled creatures, and insufficient free slots are rejected. Keep the net in your backpack and use it within its targeting range. [GH837](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1154115090975572129) [GH5401](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1548065054115766364) [C03](SOURCE_REVIEW.md#c03-taming-and-nets).

### Q118. Do more Taming skill or lower creature health improve the net roll?

**Source check: no.** The roll compares the creature's pre-capture minimum taming requirement with an integer from 50 through 200. Player skill and target health are not factors in that roll. An eligible failed attempt has a 50% chance to destroy the net; success consumes it. For requirement `T`, success is `clamp(floor(T)-49, 0, 151) / 151`. Thus a harder-to-tame eligible creature can be easier to net. [GH5401](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1548065054115766364) [C03](SOURCE_REVIEW.md#c03-taming-and-nets).

### Q119. What happens to the creature's requirements after net capture?

The current net code changes a minimum taming requirement above 29 to **29.1**, sets the owner/follow order, and bonds the pet. It adds two slots and does not run the ordinary first-tame skill/stat reduction routine in that branch. It also does not promise to heal the creature or clear all existing conditions. [GH5401](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1548065054115766364) [C03](SOURCE_REVIEW.md#c03-taming-and-nets).
