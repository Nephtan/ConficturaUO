**A player knowledge base extracted from three Discord histories, through October 10, 2026.**

The replacement adds 4,743 older game-help messages and includes all 714 messages from the first export; those 714 are counted once. See the [source review](SOURCE_REVIEW.md) for dates, coverage, hashes, and limitations.

## The most important findings

**The hardest transition is becoming self-sufficient.** Several beginners describe a loop of inadequate starting equipment, unavailable shop stock, poison, corpse recovery, and skill penalties. The useful response is a small working survival setup and one reachable activity, with recovery help when necessary. “Read everything” becomes useful when paired with the exact menu, NPC, sign, or book to read next. **Many apparent bugs are missing explanations; some are real bugs.** Rotating vendors, clone corpses, real-skill slot requirements, and different pedestal types need clear instructions. Disappearing artifact boxes, inaccessible ghost exits, misleading vendor coordinates, and unexpected skill loss need investigation rather than automatic reassurance. **The most expensive knowledge arrives too late.** Players learn about resurrection choices after losing skills, about book-binding after losing access to spells, about trash-barrel behavior after losing equipment, and about irreversible artifact spending after clicking. Put those lessons before the first dungeon or upgrade. ## How to use this collection

The starter guide and FAQ are suitable foundations for player documentation. The response guide and corrections register are particularly useful to moderators and maintainers. Replies marked as suggested wording are newly written summaries, not quotations attributed to a community member.

This is a review of exported text with selective source checks, not a certification of every mechanic on the live shard. It does not reproduce the raw chats, account details, or unrelated personal conversation. Images, linked documents, unavailable thread bodies, and attachment contents were not comprehensively inspected; their important gaps are recorded in the source review. Discord citations require access to the server.
