# What we wish we had known when starting Confictura

**Spoiler-light starting advice from the Discord histories.** For detailed mechanics, use the [FAQ](PLAYER_FAQ.md). This is a practical route through the documented learning curve, not a required build or a promise of live-server availability.

## Before leaving the starting area

**Read the tarot choices and race information.** A larger skill cap can come with less starting equipment, no Luck benefit, stronger death penalties, or permanent trouble with town guards. Staff recommend a standard start for learning. Also read the PvP choice: Neutral is not PvE protection. [G28181](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1195117369396305972) [G28184](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1195118373516869723) [GH894](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1155146005780627567)

**Find the three menus you will use constantly:** HELP, its Library, and INFO. Talk to ordinary NPCs through their context menu. Open lore books when you find them so the information is saved. The Library is part of your progress, not just flavor text. [GH2478](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1353823547461603358) [G38879](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1273413962641440910)

**Choose a small working kit.** A melee weapon with bandages, a modest tame with healing/support, or a working Elementalist setup are all represented in the logs. The important word is *working*: having a skill without its book, tool, supplies, or means of recovery leaves a new character stranded. [GH2453](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1353607732359594035) [GH2456](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1353727082802446459) [GH4712](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1454307475833749637) [GH4713](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1454307699868176534)

## Your first session

| Step | Do this | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| Get oriented | Find a bank, relevant trainers, a food/water source, and the town exit on the World Map. | You need a place to recover and sell before you need a distant dungeon. |
| Build a little resilience | Train Camping and initial useful skills; use available beginner training equipment. | Early stats and basic healing matter more than a perfect endgame template. |
| Earn a small reserve | Try cotton/flax, appropriate resources, simple crafting, or manageable combat. | Buy supplies and leave resurrection money in the bank. |
| Prepare for traps | Carry a ten-foot pole; develop Remove Trap; add a self-cast trap-removal orb when available. | Even weak dungeons contain hazards unrelated to monster strength. |
| Take one short expedition | Choose a beginner area near a town and turn back while you still have supplies. | A successful return teaches more than several unrecoverable deaths. |
| Finish deliberately | Bank valuables, feed/stable pets, and schedule an appropriate study book before logout. | You protect progress and can make offline skill gains. |

This sequence synthesizes the starting, vendor, pet, and training conversations. The precise supplies depend on your character. ## Fifteen lessons that prevent expensive mistakes

1. **A vendor's empty stock is often normal.** Each NPC rotates what it sells and buys, roughly every two hours. Try another town before assuming the item does not exist. [GH1235](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1215290842877788220) [GH4392](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1435708524285001811)

2. **Resurrection has several different costs.** Read the offer. Keep banked gold, know where the appropriate healer/shrine is, and ask for help before repeatedly resurrecting into the same enemies. Alien starts have additional penalties. [GH911](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1155428669733490738) [GH1044](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1163214607293370479) [Source check](SOURCE_REVIEW.md#c02-resurrection-penalties).

3. **Your corpse usually lasts about a day.** You often have time to arrange recovery. That does not prevent creatures from looting it or thieves from disappearing with possessions. [G21138](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1149378139139608697) [GH4200](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1425679127297392771)

4. **Poison is a major early hurdle.** Fight manageable enemies while developing healing and a cure option. Do not test every unknown dungeon snack or potion on a fragile character. [GH4425](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1436728325107089610) [GH4446](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1437074269564698828) [GH4503](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1441906666839998714)

5. **Trap protection is a toolkit.** A pole, Remove Trap, an orb, and Luck provide separate chances on supported traps. “125 means immune” and “the percentages add up” are both misleading. [GH5111](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1531328632331698187) [Trap guide](../wiki/Traps_And_Avoidance.md).

6. **Do not casually discard books and clues.** Many quests are not tracked in `[quests`. Read and save information, including the NPC or location to which you must return. [G14807](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1142082262507200583) [G7503](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1090620551917228104) [GH4410](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1436134137902661735)

7. **A mysterious portal can be a commitment.** Skara Brae is a repeated example of entering before understanding how to leave. Ask how long the adventure is and whether you can return before clicking. [GH225](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1116669144058839050) [GH269](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1117277303882387496) [GH4507](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1442253429819834420)

8. **Real skill and bonus skill are different budgets.** Rings can improve performance, but extra pet slots need all four relevant skills at their real thresholds. A power scroll raises a cap; it does not fill it. [GH1566](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1220039762325540874) [GH3398](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1393044252316926043)

9. **Feed and stable your pets.** Bonding is not permission to ignore loyalty. Build an affordable working pack before taking an irreplaceable unbonded pet into danger. [GH202](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1113888803317350520) [GH838](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1154115308114681967) [GH3570](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1398415172376330421)

10. **A throwing net is a valuable consumable with a slot cost.** It adds two slots to the captured pet. Your Taming skill does not improve the actual capture roll in the checked code. [GH5401](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1548065054115766364) [Source check](SOURCE_REVIEW.md#c03-taming-and-nets).

11. **Similar-looking treasure can obey different rules.** Quest pedestals, stealing pedestals, decorative valuables, unidentified gear, and mysterious artifact boxes are not one system. [GH945](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1155970337406717953) [GH4356](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1432787087471677643) [GH2583](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1359192668625965128)

12. **Cancel may only close the upgrade window.** Decide how to spend artifact points first. Apply item transformations in a considered order, particularly pearls and horseshoes. [GS213](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1063677605192138842/1540680650443587686) [GH454](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1127667518421606521) [GH459](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1128327990850363532)

13. **Keep your trash barrel away from active storage work.** Players report whole containers being deleted after the barrel auto-emptied. There was no promised undo. This is an avoidable loss even inside a house. [G45971](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1553961996502962187) [G45974](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1553962160508502057) [G46019](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1060698288401154159/1553976524904996936)

14. **A character wandering town may be an NPC clone.** You can sometimes hire or train from it, but its copied equipment cannot be looted. Its presence does not prove the human player is online. [GH1117](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1173845542657347615) [GH1118](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1173845704582639668) [GH4554](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1442673888520835283)

15. **Ask for the amount of help you want.** “One hint, no destination spoilers” is a useful request. So is “I am stuck and want the full route out.” Give your location and what you tried. [GH3929](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1406933364577009684) [GH4432](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1436817506198356221) [GH4433](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1436837503247192085)

## If the first session goes badly

Stop the immediate loss loop. Bank what remains, get safe, and ask for one concrete assist: a corpse escort, a working weapon/book, directions to a buyer, or a short demonstration. You do not need to optimize every skill before you can play. The logs include both frustrated starts and players later reporting that a beginner dungeon or guided clue got them moving. [GH2453](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1353607732359594035) [GH2478](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1353823547461603358) [GH4413](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1436333856423677983) [GH4507](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1442253429819834420)

Suggested request:

> I'm new and playing a standard [build]. I'm at [town/facet]. I can currently [what works], but I'm stuck on [one problem]. I've tried [steps]. Could someone give me [a hint / a quick demonstration / an escort]? I would like [minimal / full] spoilers.

For a death, add the dungeon, floor, coordinates/landmark, approximate corpse age, and whether you are still online. Avoid posting account passwords or saved client credentials.
