# Major player struggles and the best responses

The priorities below reflect **severity, recurrence across conversations, and how directly better guidance could help**. They are editorial priorities, not measured percentages of players. A long conversation can be one person's incident; repeated quotes and bot replies are not independent complaints. Suggested replies are newly written, ready-to-adapt wording, not quotations from staff.

## Priority overview

| Priority | Struggle | What the player needs first |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate | Death, poison, and repeated skill loss | A survivable recovery route and an explanation of resurrection costs |
| Immediate | Trapped ghost, inaccessible corpse, catastrophic item loss | Rescue/investigation, precise expectations, and recovery help |
| Immediate | First character cannot function | A working starter kit and one manageable activity |
| High | Cannot buy tools/books or sell gathered resources | The rotating-stock rule plus a specific alternative shop |
| High | Does not know what to do next | A named menu, clue, sign, or nearby objective |
| High | Trap effects feel arbitrary | A small defensive routine and diagnosis of the actual effect |
| High | Pets disobey, disappear, or attack allies | Real-skill thresholds, loyalty management, and aggression recovery |
| High | Quest object behaves unlike an ordinary item | Identify which quest/item system it belongs to |
| High | Conflicting mechanics and outdated answers | A dated correction with the earlier claim explicitly retired |
| Medium | Build/cap anxiety | Explain total cap, individual cap, and effective skill separately |
| Medium | Client/mobile/assistant failures | Platform/package details and reversible troubleshooting |
| Medium | Housing/crafting tools are unfamiliar | Correct tool/menu/category and a safe demonstration |
| Medium | Travel locks and return routes | Explain route restrictions before the trip |
| Medium | Sparse population at the player's hours | A scheduled handoff/escort and asynchronous instructions |

## 1. The first-death spiral

**Evidence.** Players describe losing skills, lacking money or spells, and being unable to survive poison while trying to rebuild. In one later incident, a player repeatedly resurrected under skeletal mages because they believed the corpse would disappear after minutes; only afterward did they learn that recovery could wait. [GH395](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1123967583247413360) [GH418](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1123970224954294384) [GH2453](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1353607732359594035) [GH4423](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1436715502683558043) [GH4194](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1425678930320293889) [GH4199](https://discord.com/channels/1060698288011104358/1071288553516048431/1425679121132032030)

**Best response.** Explain the available resurrection methods before discussing an optimized build. Establish whether the character is standard or Alien, whether funds are banked, and whether a rescuer can reach the body. Offer a low-risk healing-training step and a cure plan. Repeatedly saying the shard is hard does not supply a recovery action.

**Suggested reply:**

> That is a rough start. Stop taking the same resurrection penalty for now. Your corpse normally lasts about 24 hours, although monsters can loot it. What town or dungeon/floor are you in, and is this a standard or Alien character? Let's arrange the appropriate healer or a corpse escort, then get you a small healing/cure kit before your next trip.

**Observed outcome.** The October 2025 player recovered their own gear but had already paid penalties and lost an item to a creature. Later beginner advice explicitly recommends weak animals for healing practice and identifies poison as the hurdle. The logs do not prove that every rescue request received help. ## Documentation and onboarding changes worth making first

These are recommendations from the review, not implemented features:

1. Put the short **resurrection, poison, and corpse-recovery explanation** before the first dungeon.
2. Add **rotating stock and buy lists** to shopping instructions and tool/book acquisition help.
3. Introduce **HELP → Library, NPC Talk, and one local clue** through a short walkthrough.
4. Explain the **commitment and return route** before unusual portals and long starter adventures.
5. Put **real skill, total cap, individual cap, and pet slots** on one comparison page.
6. Show **quest pedestal versus stealing pedestal**, using current screenshots when available.
7. Warn before **irreversible artifact spending, item transformations, and bulk trash disposal**.
8. Keep a dated **known issues/corrections list**, especially for ghost escape, artifact boxes, vendor coordinates, and client packages.

Success could be measured by fewer repeated clarification loops, more first-session returns from a dungeon, successful rescues, and fewer avoidable losses. The supplied logs alone do not establish those rates.
